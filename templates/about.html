Vossome Window Cleaning is a locally owned St. Charles company founded by Matt Voss and shaped by family, three generations of window-cleaning experience, and one simple belief: we’re in the Happy Client Business.

That belief became the Vossome standard. It means clear communication, dependable service, respect for every property, and work the company is proud to put its name on. Customers should know what to expect from Vossome no matter who answers the phone or arrives for the job.

The family story explains where the company came from, while the way Vossome operates carries it forward. We clean windows, refresh exterior surfaces, and clear gutters throughout St. Charles and nearby St. Louis communities with a process designed to feel friendly, professional, and easy to recommend.

Being local also means contributing locally. Giving back to organizations like The Covering House is part of doing business #likeaVoss—and every clean, bright result should feel unmistakably #awesomeVossome.
